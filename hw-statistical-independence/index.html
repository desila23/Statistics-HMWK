Statistical independence.

Does knowing X change the distribution of F? Build a contingency table, compare conditional frequencies and test how far the observed counts depart from independence.

03 / Compare with independence

Pearson’s chi-square statistic.

H₀: X and F are independent in the population. H₁: at least one joint probability differs from the product of its margins.

Expected counts

Eij=RiCjN

Under H₀, the fitted joint relative frequency is (Rᵢ/N)(Cⱼ/N). Multiply by N to obtain Eᵢⱼ. Expected counts can be fractional and preserve both observed margins.

Sum the departures

χ2=∑i,j(Oij−Eij)2Eij

Degrees of freedom = (r−1)(c−1). Starting with rc−1 free cell probabilities, fitting the row and column margins uses (r−1)+(c−1) parameters. This implementation uses Pearson’s statistic without Yates’ continuity correction.

This assumption concerns independence between observations, not the H₀ relationship between X and F. Frequency counts must represent actual mutually exclusive observations, not arbitrary survey weights. Check the study design before using inferential conclusions.

What this dataset supports

Which cells contribute?

Inspect observed minus expected and the Pearson residual (O−E)/√E. Squaring the residual gives that cell’s contribution to χ². These diagnostics locate departures; they are not separate cell-level significance tests.

04 / Probability and statistics

Independence is a model property.

Independence in probability

Random variables X and F are independent if P(X ∈ A, F ∈ B) = P(X ∈ A)P(F ∈ B) for every pair of measurable sets A and B. For categorical variables, checking every category pair is enough.

When P(X = xᵢ) > 0, independence implies P(F = fⱼ | X = xᵢ) = P(F = fⱼ). Conditional probabilities with a zero-probability conditioning event are not defined by this ratio.

Empirical independence vs a test

In the empirical distribution, exact independence means Oᵢⱼ/N = (Rᵢ/N)(Cⱼ/N) for every cell. The lab checks this using integer products, before rounding. Real samples can depart from this equality even when the population is independent.

A hypothesis test asks whether sampling variation plausibly explains the observed departure. A small p-value is evidence against H₀ under the test assumptions. A large p-value means failure to reject H₀; it does not prove independence or give the probability that H₀ is true.

When is the approximation useful?

The right-tail p-value uses a chi-square distribution with (r−1)(c−1) degrees of freedom. Its large-sample justification requires independent observations and adequate expected counts. As a conservative rule, this lab reports a p-value only when every expected count is at least 5.

For sparse tables, consider Fisher’s exact test for 2×2 data or a suitable exact/Monte Carlo method. These alternatives are not implemented here. Do not merge categories just to obtain a desired result. Structural zeros, repeated measurements and complex survey designs require other models.

Association is not causation

A relationship between X and F may reflect confounding, selection, reverse causation or a common cause. For example, training status and alerts could both depend on job role or system exposure.

A contingency table alone cannot show that changing X would change F. A causal claim needs a defensible study design, relevant assumptions and control of competing explanations. In a constructed teaching example, even a tiny p-value is not evidence about actual security outcomes.

05 / Conclusions

Describe first. Infer with care.

The margins summarize each variable; conditional frequencies expose differences across groups. Expected counts translate independence into a prediction with the same margins. Pearson’s statistic measures departures and its p-value is conditional on an appropriate sampling model. Statistical significance, effect size and causality answer different questions.

Small samples can miss meaningful associations. Large samples can make small departures statistically significant. Cramér’s V provides a descriptive magnitude on a 0–1 scale, without direction or a universal threshold for practical importance.
